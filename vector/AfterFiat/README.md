# AfterFiat — Next-Gen Store of Value: Privacy, Proofs, Compute

Sole-author, versioned monetary research thesis by Jason St George, published at https://afterfiat.xyz (profile: https://jasonstgeorge.com/afterfiat).

`NextGenStoreofValue.pdf` in this folder is **v3.2** (released 2026-09-13), downloaded from https://afterfiat.xyz/pdf/next-gen-sov-v3.2.pdf. It is the source of truth for anything the thesis says; earlier summaries of v1.x (five red lines, 31 sections, appendices A–G) are superseded.

## Current release

- **Version:** 3.2, "the propagation pass". A second whole-thesis review found that v3.1 applied every earlier finding at its site but left summaries and definitions behind. v3.2 brings them into line with the body, supplies two promised tables, and adds rows for the three link-9 instruments. No threshold moved, no red line was relaxed, no mechanism was added.
- **Structure:** §0–§33 (34 sections) and appendices A–J.
- **Argument:** ten premises, a nine-link chain, eighteen red lines (each specified so it can be failed), and four telemetry families across Privacy, Proofs, Compute, and market realization.
- **Previous release:** v3.1 (2026-09-11), the peer-review remediation pass: forty-nine external findings applied, keyed citations, and Kardashev Labs, a proposed build-and-measure institution with its own kill conditions.
