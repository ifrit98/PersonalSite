# AfterFiat — Next-Gen Store of Value: Privacy, Proofs, Compute

Sole-author, versioned monetary research thesis by Jason St George, published at https://afterfiat.xyz (profile: https://jasonstgeorge.com/afterfiat).

`NextGenStoreofValue.pdf` in this folder is **v3.4** (released 2026-09-27, 556 pages), downloaded from https://afterfiat.xyz/pdf/next-gen-sov-v3.4.pdf. It is the source of truth for anything the thesis says; earlier summaries (ten premises, §0–§33, v1.x's five red lines and appendices A–G) are superseded.

## Current release

- **Version:** 3.4, "the rule-of-its-own pass". v3.3 named the buyer of verification; v3.4 turns the same attention on the buyer of the reserve asset, shows that the automatic bid and the leveraged residual are one chain, and makes the book read as one document: contradictions resolved in the stronger register, the conclusion ending once, and one commentator cited where his ideas are adopted and nowhere else. No threshold moved, no red line relaxed, no rating raised, no layer added.
- **Previous release:** v3.3 (2026-09-20), "the delegation-economy pass", added the chapter *The Delegation Economy: Who Buys Verification*, which models the buyer of verification as one machine process handing a task to another at a speed that admits no due diligence.
- **Structure:** six parts, 35 sections, and appendices A–J. The web edition numbers sections §0–§33 plus §5b (the Delegation Economy); the PDF numbers them 1–35, so section numbers differ between the two editions.
- **Argument:** eleven premises, a nine-link chain, a seven-layer stack, eighteen red lines (each specified so it can be failed), and four telemetry families, VerifyFlow among them, distinguishing monetary adoption from financialization.
